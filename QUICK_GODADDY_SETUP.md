## ✅ **What We've Done:**
- ✅ Added GoDaddy SMTP integration to your CRM

## 📧 **Step 1: Buy GoDaddy Email (2 minutes)**

1. **Go to GoDaddy.com** → Login to your account
2. **Find billbytekot.in** in "My Products"
3. **Click "Email"** → **"Email Essentials"** ($1.99/month)
4. **Create these mailboxes:**
   - `support@billbytekot.in`
   - `info@billbytekot.in`
   - `noreply@billbytekot.in`

## ⚙️ **Step 2: Configure in Your CRM (1 minute)**

1. **Go to your CRM** → **Settings** → **Integrations**
2. **Find "GoDaddy Email (SMTP)"**
3. **Click "Connect"**
4. **Fill in these details:**
   ```
   SMTP Host: smtpout.secureserver.net (pre-filled)
   Port: 587 (pre-filled)
   Email Address: support@billbytekot.in
   Email Password: [your GoDaddy email password]
   From Email: support@billbytekot.in
   From Name: Pulse CRM Support
   ```
5. **Click "Save"**

## 🧪 **Step 3: Test (1 minute)**

1. **Click "Test"** button in the integration
2. **Should show "OK"** if working
3. **Go to Emails** → **Compose** → **Send test email**
4. **Check it arrives from support@billbytekot.in**
